# ByteDance OmniHuman 2.0

`bytedance/omnihuman/2.0` · video · Bytedance

OmniHuman 2.0 by ByteDance turns a single image and an audio track into a talking or performing video that follows the audio.

## Pricing

- Depends on parameters (`params.audio`, `usage.duration`).

Formula: `(usage.duration ?? media_duration(params.audio) ?? 5.0) * 0.105`

## Call it

## Details

OmniHuman 2.0 turns a single image and an audio track into a talking or performing video, with lip sync, gestures and expressions that follow the audio.
### Fields
| Field | Type | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `image` | image URL, required | — | Person or character to animate. 300–6000 px per side, aspect ratio 0.4–2.5, up to 30 MB. |
| `audio` | audio URL, required | — | Speech or song, WAV or MP3, 4–300 seconds, up to 15 MB. |
| `prompt` | string | — | Optional direction for scene, gestures and camera. |
### Billing
$0.105 per output second; the output is as long as the audio. The quote before submission assumes 5 seconds when the audio length cannot be read.
| Audio | Price |
|---|---|
| 5s audio | $0.525 |
| 30s audio | $3.15 |
| 60s audio | $6.30 |
Failed runs are not charged.
### Prompting
- Describe gestures and camera; the lips follow the audio automatically.
### Limits
- Audio must be 4–300 seconds.
